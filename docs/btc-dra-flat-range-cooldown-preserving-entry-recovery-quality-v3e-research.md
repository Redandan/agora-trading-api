## Recovery labels

Labels are outcomes, never input features. For every unfiltered V3 filled lot,
find the first complete hourly bar strictly after fill at which fee- and
slippage-adjusted estimated net PnL is strictly positive.

- `RAPID_RECOVERY`: first strict net-positive occurs within `168` hours.
- `INTERMEDIATE_RECOVERY`: first strict net-positive occurs after `168` and
  within `720` hours.
- `LONG_UNDERWATER`: first strict net-positive does not occur within `720`
  hours.
- `RIGHT_CENSORED`: fewer than `720` observable hours remain before the frozen
  window end and no earlier label can be assigned.

The `168`-hour boundary is the unchanged seven-day cooldown horizon. The
`720`-hour boundary is the unchanged 30-day arm/expiry horizon. Design,
Validation, and every annual fold are labelled only with observations inside
their own window; future windows cannot complete an earlier window's label.

## Cooldown-preserving counterfactual

The raw reservation calendar is generated by the frozen V3 entry signal plus
the unchanged arm/expiry and seven-day cooldown. On every raw reservation:

1. advance `lastEntrySignal` to the reservation time;
2. clear the active arm;
3. evaluate the selected quality factor;
4. if the factor fails, log a quality rejection and create no pending buy;
5. if it passes, apply cap accounting and the unchanged next-open fill/gap
   rule.

Both quality rejection and capacity rejection consume the reservation. This
keeps the raw reservation times identical across the unfiltered baseline,
every factor, and the fixed-calendar one-slot diagnostic. Required audits:

- exact reservation-time equality across modes;
- every adjacent reservation respects the seven-day cooldown;
- every quality rejection advanced the cooldown clock;
- factor truth exactly reconciles accepted versus quality-rejected signals;
- accepted fills are a subset of unfiltered V3 fills;
- entry, exit, positivity, and accounting audits all pass.
