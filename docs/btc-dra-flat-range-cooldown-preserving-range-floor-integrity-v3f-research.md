## Causal range-floor state

At every signal, use complete UTC days strictly before the current signal day.

```text
currentFloor = minimum low of days [-20, -1]
precedingFloor = minimum low of days [-40, -21]
floorDay = latest day in [-20, -1] whose low equals currentFloor
floorAgeCompleteDays = signalUtcDate - floorDayUtcDate
```

Choosing the latest matching minimum is conservative when the exact floor is
retested. All 40 days must be present. The current incomplete UTC day is never
used.
