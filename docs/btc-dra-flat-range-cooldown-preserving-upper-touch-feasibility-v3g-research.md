## Upper-touch outcome labels

For every unfiltered V3 filled lot, find the first complete hourly bar strictly
after fill whose close is at least its entry-frozen upper third.

- `TIMELY_UPPER_TOUCH`: first touch occurs within `720` hours.
- `LATE_UPPER_TOUCH_AFTER_720H`: first observed touch occurs after `720`
  hours. This is a 720-hour failure.
- `NO_UPPER_TOUCH_WITHIN_720H`: no touch by 720 hours and the complete
  720-hour horizon is observable inside the frozen window.
- `RIGHT_CENSORED`: no touch is observed and fewer than 720 hours remain
  before the frozen window end.

The `720`-hour boundary is the unchanged 30-day arm/expiry horizon. Design,
Validation, and annual labels use only bars inside their own windows.


current incomplete UTC day is not used in the 20-day range. The signal close,
